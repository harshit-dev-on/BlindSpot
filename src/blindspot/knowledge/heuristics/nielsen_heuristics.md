# Nielsen-Norman 10 Usability Heuristics Playbook

1. **Visibility of System Status**
   - The design should always keep users informed about what is going on, through appropriate feedback within a reasonable time.
   - *Friction points:* Clicking a button with no loading spinner, long multi-second pauses with no progress indication.

2. **Match Between System and the Real World**
   - The design should speak the users' language, using words, phrases, and concepts familiar to the user rather than internal tech jargon.
   - *Friction points:* Cryptic error codes ("ERR_NULL_PTR"), unnatural form fields.

3. **User Control and Freedom**
   - Users often make mistakes and need a clearly marked "emergency exit" to leave the unwanted state without having to go through an extended process.
   - *Friction points:* No "Cancel" or "Back" button in a modal, irreversible deletion without confirmation, forced workflows.

4. **Consistency and Standards**
   - Users should not have to wonder whether different words, situations, or actions mean the same thing. Follow platform and industry conventions.
   - *Friction points:* Unpredictable button colors (green for delete), links that don't look clickable.

5. **Error Prevention**
   - Better than good error messages is a careful design which prevents a problem from occurring in the first place.
   - *Friction points:* Missing inline validation, allowing invalid characters in phone/zip fields and only complaining after full page submit.

6. **Recognition Rather than Recall**
   - Minimize the user's memory load by making elements, actions, and options visible.
   - *Friction points:* Hidden pricing information, multi-step forms where prior choices disappear from view.

7. **Flexibility and Efficiency of Use**
   - Shortcuts — hidden from novice users — may speed up the interaction for the expert user.
   - *Friction points:* Repetitive manual input, no auto-fill, inability to paste into sensitive fields.

8. **Aesthetic and Minimalist Design**
   - Interfaces should not contain information which is irrelevant or rarely needed.
   - *Friction points:* Cluttered dashboards, walls of dense unformatted text, aggressive banners obscuring content.

9. **Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors**
   - Error messages should be expressed in plain language (no error codes), precisely indicate the problem, and constructively suggest a solution.
   - *Friction points:* Generic "Submission failed" errors with no field indication.

10. **Help and Documentation**
    - Even though it is better if the system can be used without documentation, it may be necessary to provide help and documentation.
    - *Friction points:* Missing tooltips for complex domain terms, no FAQ or contact link when stuck.
